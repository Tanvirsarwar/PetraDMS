## SKU Naming Standard

The new SKU format follows:

`BRAND-PRODUCT-TYPE-SIZE`

Examples:

- `MARKS-PM-2KG`
- `AMA-MILK-500G`
- `AMA-COFFEE-MIX-1KG`
- `TEA-500G`
- `MPP-AS-400G`
- `SHAH-NOODLES-12PK`

### Important

- **AURA products are intentionally excluded.**
- SKU codes use **uppercase letters**.
- Spaces are replaced with `-`.
- Product size is standardized as `KG`, `G`, `ML`, `L`, or `PK`.
- These SKUs are designed to be easy to search, scan, type, and maintain in a POS/inventory database.
